# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indie game developers, pixel artists, and digital creators working on 2D games, retro sprites, and creative assets. They operate on Linux workstations (often laptop GPUs like RTX 3050 4GB) and need a reliable, native, zero-latency tool to synthesize game-ready sprites, isolate clean alpha cutouts, curate custom prompt recipes, and pipe directly into editors like Aseprite.

## Product Purpose

Lemonade Studio exists to deliver a lightning-fast, tactile, distraction-free creative environment for AI image synthesis, background removal, and style orchestration. Success means an artist can draft or prompt an asset, dial in LoRA weights or reference imagery, cleanly strip backgrounds, and export into their asset pipeline in seconds without fighting sluggish cloud web UIs or memory bloat.

## Brand Personality

- **Voice & Tone:** Bold, Tactile, Playful. High-energy creative confidence with Gumroad and Teenage Engineering warmth.
- **Three-Word Essence:** Bold · Tactile · Irreverent.
- **Emotional Goals:** Empowerment, kinetic satisfaction ("juice"), delight, and effortless creative flow.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI SaaS:** Soft pastel beige/cream washes, washed-out low-contrast gray text, faux-sophisticated blur gradients, and corporate AI marketing clichés.
- **Microscopic DAW / Cluttered IDE:** Endless sub-menus, tiny unreadable sliders, and opaque technical abbreviations that stall creative momentum.
- **Sterile Utility:** Bland default desktop widgets lacking visual rhythm, personality, or tactile feedback.

## Design Principles

1. **Kinetic Tactility First:** Every button press, toggle, and generation provides clear physical feedback (punchy recoil, crisp chimes, decisive outlines).
2. **Respect the Artist's Flow:** Fast round-trips from generation to cutout to Aseprite export with zero modal friction or hidden options.
3. **Intentional Geometry:** Unapologetic solid borders, hard offset shadows, high-legibility display typography, and punchy signature accents.
4. **Honest Hardware Awareness:** Transparent feedback on model readiness, VRAM conservation, offline procedural fallbacks, and local execution.
5. **Full Sensory Autonomy:** Deliberate juice (screen shake, CRT overlays, paper noise textures, sound cues) that users can dial up, customize, or silence at will.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- High-contrast visual structure (minimum 2px solid outlines, stark foreground-to-background contrast meeting WCAG AA standards across both light and dark modes).
- Full user control over kinetic feedback: toggleable paper texture overlay, optional screen shake, toggleable CRT phosphor scanlines, and adjustable chime volume.
- Respect for reduced-motion preferences with snappy/instant UI transitions and full keyboard shortcut accessibility.
