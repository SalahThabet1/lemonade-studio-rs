## What Changed

1. **Eliminated Stacked Double Headers**: Removed nested 52–56px headers from `standalone_rmbg.rs` and `style_library.rs`. Promoted contextual space actions ("Open Image...", "Save Studio Prompt", "New Recipe") directly into the global `render_header`. Reclaimed ~54px of vertical height across both screens.
2. **De-duplicated Preview Toolbar**: Replaced echoed resolution, steps, and CFG chips in `preview_panel.rs` with clean canvas viewport labeling and zoom mode indicators (`Nearest-Neighbor` / `Smooth Zoom`).
3. **Progressive Disclosure on DiffSynth Controls**: Collapsed advanced block slicing (`all` vs `1:180:2`), merge modes (`mean` vs `sum`), and negative template toggles behind an "Advanced Architecture ▼/▲" disclosure accordion.
4. **Added Power Keyboard Accelerators**: Registered `Ctrl+Enter` for generation, `Ctrl+1/2/3` for quick space navigation, and `Escape` for modal dismissal.
5. **Fixed Nav Rail Icon Collision**: Changed brand logo badge to `IconName::Zap`, eliminating visual duplication with the adjacent `Studio` space icon (`Sparkles`).
