## Overall Impression

Lemonade Studio possesses a bold, energetic Neubrutalist personality that feels tactile and enjoyable to use. The instant feedback, punchy borders, and seamless theme accents feel right at home on an indie developer's desktop. However, the application currently suffers from **scaffolding duplication**: sub-spaces render duplicate nested headers beneath the main application header, and metadata chips are echoed across stacked bars, creating unnecessary visual weight.

---

## What's Working

1. **Unapologetic Neubrutalist Identity**: Solid 2px outlines, sharp geometric tags, tactile risograph paper noise, and dynamic signature accents (Gumroad Pop Yellow, Acid Lime, Bubblegum Magenta, Bauhaus Cobalt) provide a memorable, cohesive aesthetic.
2. **Game-Dev Focused Workflow**: Direct 1-click piping between generation, background removal, and Aseprite export with 1-bit crisp pixel alpha mode respects the creator's real-world pipeline.
3. **Responsive Visual History**: The bottom history filmstrip reel gives immediate reassurance and allows switching between recent generations without leaving the canvas.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Nested Double Headers in Sub-Spaces
- **What**: Both `BackgroundRemover` (`standalone_rmbg.rs`) and `StyleLibrary` (`style_library.rs`) render an internal 52–56px top header directly below the global `render_header`.
- **Why it matters**: Consumes 100+ pixels of vertical screen height on laptop screens, duplicates space titles, and places duplicate action buttons ("New Recipe", "Change Source") in two stacked rows.
- **Fix**: Elevate space actions into the global `render_header` contextual utility slot and remove the internal secondary headers from `standalone_rmbg.rs` and `style_library.rs`.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable layout` / `$impeccable distill`

### [P1] Redundant Echoed Metadata Chips
- **What**: In the Studio view, resolution (`1024×1024`), steps (`30 Steps`), and CFG scale (`CFG 7.0`) are rendered in the top header and immediately repeated 52 pixels below in the preview panel toolbar.
- **Why it matters**: Creates visual noise and wastes toolbar space that could otherwise house zoom controls, aspect-ratio presets, or a canvas clear button.
- **Fix**: Keep the summary pills in the top header breadcrumb and dedicate the preview toolbar exclusively to canvas actions (Zoom, Fit, RMBG, Save, Open).
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable distill`

### [P1] DiffSynth Controls Lack Progressive Disclosure
- **What**: Technical fine-tuning knobs (odd transformer block slices `1:180:2`, merge mode `Mean/Sum`, negative template gray switches) are permanently expanded in the control sidebar.
- **Why it matters**: Pushes the prompt and generation button further down, causing high extraneous cognitive load for artists who just want to dial in style influence.
- **Fix**: Group advanced block slicing and merge math into a collapsible "Advanced Architecture" disclosure accordion.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable shape` / `$impeccable layout`

### [P2] Missing Global Keyboard Accelerators
- **What**: Common power actions require explicit mouse clicks.
- **Why it matters**: Breaks the flow for pixel artists working with tablets or keyboard-heavy shortcuts.
- **Fix**: Bind `Ctrl+Enter` to trigger generation, `Ctrl+1/2/3` to switch spaces, and `Escape` to close active modals.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable polish` / `$impeccable harden`

## Minor Observations

- The left rail brand logo badge uses the `Sparkles` icon, colliding with the adjacent `Studio` navigation icon. Replacing the badge with a distinctive logo glyph (such as a citrus lemon or bolt) clarifies identity.
- The Settings dialog has an 860×640 fixed box layout that could feel constrained on compact displays.

## Questions to Consider

- What if sub-spaces fed their unique actions directly into the main application top bar, completely abolishing secondary nested headers?
- What if the preview panel toolbar was upgraded with canvas zoom presets (`100%`, `200%`, `Fit`) instead of duplicating resolution and step chips?
